Stranger Things Composition

A detailed recreation of Joyce Byers' living room featuring Lumen lighting and creature integration in UE5.

Unreal Engine 5 • Lumen • Environment Art

Technical Insights

Focusing on the 1980s aesthetic, I utilized Lumen for the Christmas light bounce. The Demogorgon was rigged and placed to create a high-contrast silhouette against the alphabet wall.

Lighting Workflow

Real-time Global Illumination (Lumen) and Volumetric Fog.

Asset Creation

Hard surface modeling in Blender, textured in Substance Painter.
