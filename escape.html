Escape: Unity to UE5

A psychological horror environment originally built in Unity, now fully reimagined in Unreal Engine 5. This study explores environment design and leverages Lumen's real-time GI.

UE5 Migration • Environment Design • Lumen

Technical Insights

After entering the game industry, we felt the need to deepen our technical expertise. We chose to recreate this Unity-based project in Unreal Engine 5 as a comprehensive masterclass. This allowed us to dissect the intricacies of Lumen and Nanite while transitioning our workflow to a high-fidelity pipeline.

Engine Migration

Transferred core environmental logic from Unity to UE5, focusing on Real-time Global Illumination and the implementation of complex shader networks to enhance material fidelity and physical accuracy.

Mastery Study

Used the reconstruction to fully harness the capabilities of the Unreal Engine 5 framework, specifically focusing on how volumetric fog and emissive materials influence light behavior in a high-tension horror context.
