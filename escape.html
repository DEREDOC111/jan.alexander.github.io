Escape: Unity to UE5

A psychological horror environment originally built in Unity, now fully reimagined in Unreal Engine 5. This study explores level design and leverages Lumen's real-time GI.

UE5 Migration • Environment Design • Lumen

Technical Migration

Translating this project from Unity to Unreal Engine 5 allowed me to replace baked lighting with dynamic Lumen Global Illumination, significantly increasing the visual tension.

Workflow Evolution

Managed the porting of assets and logic while refining the environmental storytelling to suit UE5's high-fidelity capabilities.

Atmospheric Design

Focused on Volumetric Lighting and tight camera angles to enhance the psychological horror experience.
